7. Short explanation of how data flows through the backend
Simulated feeds in weather.py, traffic.py, and incidents.py generate city-zone civic readings.
The normalization layer in normalizer.py converts feeds into one common schema.
ingestion.py validates, saves the rows to SQLite, and triggers anomaly/correlation analysis.
anomaly.py applies explainable thresholds such as high rainfall or heavy congestion.
correlation.py checks for co-occurring signals in the same zone and 
time window without claiming causation.
health_score.py computes a 0–100 composite city/zone health score.
dashboard.py aggregates the results into a frontend-ready response, and summary.py generates the plain-language summary.
The system is intentionally designed to keep working even if one feed is missing or delayed. It fails gracefully, rather than crashing the entire dashboard.
